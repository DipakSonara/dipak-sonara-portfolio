01 / About

A decade in iOS. Ten years inside banks. Now cross-platform and AI-first.

Mobile engineer with 15 years of experience, built on more than a decade in the iOS ecosystem and extended into Flutter for cross-platform delivery.

I've spent my career in service-based companies, shipping apps for clients in banking, insurance, healthcare and e-commerce. I own the full journey: requirement gathering, architecture, development, testing and release on the App Store.

For the last ten years my focus has been digital banking. On the Backbase platform, I've helped deliver two digital banking apps: Bineo, a digital bank in Mexico, and SNB Digital for Saudi National Bank. All of it was built in Swift with the Backbase SDK, using the BB design system and BB Flow SDK for onboarding.

I also spent two and a half years building a consumer fintech app in Flutter, shipping to iOS and Android from a single codebase for a client under NDA. It covered banking, payments, KYC onboarding and lending journeys.

I work AI-first. I use Claude Code, Cursor and GitHub Copilot every day to scaffold, refactor, test and document code, while keeping architecture, security and final review in my own hands. I'm keen to grow into building generative AI features into products.

I'm open to any technology role, remote or with relocation. I bring a strong work ethic and a commitment to shipping high-quality, secure products.
